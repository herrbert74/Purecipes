Delete your Purecipes account

Last updated: 25 July 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Purecipes account and the personal data associated with it. Purecipes is provided by Zsolt Bertalan. For privacy details, see our Privacy Policy.

1. Delete your account in the Purecipes app

If you still have the app installed:

  1. Open Purecipes and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to your profile / account screen.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm deletion when prompted.

This permanently removes your Purecipes sign-in account and the account data we hold for you, and signs you out. The action cannot be undone.

2. Request deletion without the app

If you have uninstalled Purecipes, cannot sign in, or prefer to request deletion by email, contact us at info@purecipes.app.

Please include:

  • the subject line “Purecipes account deletion request”;
  • the email address used for your Purecipes account; and
  • the sign-in method you used (email and password, Google, Apple, or Facebook), if you know it.

We may ask you to confirm that you control that email address before we delete the account. We aim to complete deletion requests within 30 days of verifying your request.

3. Data we delete

When your Purecipes account is deleted, we delete or anonymise personal data associated with that account, including:

  • account profile details such as display name, email address, and profile image URL;
  • authentication / sign-in credentials for Purecipes;
  • favourites and cookbooks linked to your account;
  • pantry, excluded-ingredient, and measurement preference settings;
  • in-app sessions for your account; and
  • other account-linked content we store to provide Purecipes features.

4. Data we may keep

Some information may be retained where needed, for example:

  • recipes you created remain available in Purecipes, and their images stay with them, so other users’ saved copies and shared content are not broken. We transfer these recipes to a Purecipes system account, which removes their association with your former account;
  • purchase, subscription, and transaction records held by Apple, Google Play, or our payment / entitlement providers, for fraud prevention, accounting, and legal obligations;
  • records we must keep for legal, security, dispute, or regulatory reasons; and
  • aggregated or anonymised analytics that no longer identify you.

Deleting your Purecipes account does not automatically cancel an active subscription. Cancel subscriptions through the Google Play Store or Apple App Store settings for your device account.

5. Contact

Questions about account deletion: info@purecipes.app.
